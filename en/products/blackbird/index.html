Blackbird

Build less tooling. Ship more games.

Blackbird turns your scripts, commands, and development workflows into a fast, organized desktop tool—without requiring you to build and maintain a custom internal application.

Run builds. Deploy to devices. Collect logs. Manage projects. Automate repetitive work. Keep everything your team needs in one place.

Your development workflows, organized

Command-line tools are powerful, but they can be difficult to remember, share, and use consistently. Blackbird gives them a clean, approachable interface with configurable actions, inputs, presets, queues, and status tracking.

Your existing scripts remain yours. Blackbird simply makes them easier to run.

Stop rebuilding the same internal tools

Small studios rarely have the time or resources to create polished internal development software. Blackbird provides the reusable interface, so you can focus on the scripts and workflows that are unique to your project.

  • No bespoke GUI for every utility.
  • No collection of forgotten batch files.
  • No searching through old documentation for the right command.

Built for game development

Blackbird is designed around the practical needs of game developers:

  • Build and package projects
  • Deploy builds to local or connected devices
  • Launch, stop, and uninstall applications
  • Capture and retrieve logs
  • Run maintenance and cleanup tasks
  • Chain repeatable workflows together
  • Expose complex tools through simple, configurable actions

Powerful enough for developers. Simple enough for the whole team.

Create actions with clearly labeled options, sensible defaults, and reusable presets.

Technical team members can build the underlying workflows, while artists, designers, producers, and testers can run them safely without memorizing command-line syntax.

Your tools. Your infrastructure.

Blackbird runs your existing local scripts and commands. It does not require you to move your projects, builds, or workflows into a hosted service.

Keep control of your development environment while making it significantly easier to use.
